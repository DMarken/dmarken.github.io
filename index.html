Private Files Tool

Private Files Tool is a private, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps the owner organize their own Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Gmail, Calendar, Tasks, Contacts, YouTube and Google Photos.

It isn't offered to the public. No one other than its owner can sign in to it, and it has no accounts, no sign-up and no server of its own.

How it uses Google data

The tool reads and changes only its owner's own Google account, and only when its owner runs it. Data moves directly between the owner's computer and Google. Nothing is sent to anyone else. See the privacy policy for details.
